Yo is back, and you can try it today: on iPhone and on Android.

**iPhone.** Join the beta on [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/V3wa1DeX), install, pick a username, tap a name. That's it.

![Yo for Android. Beta's open. 1 Join group, 2 Become a tester, 3 Install.](/img/changelog/android-beta.png)

**Android.** Google Play needs three taps for a beta:

1. Join the testers: [groups.google.com/g/yo-android-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/yo-android-testers)
2. Become a tester: [play.google.com/apps/testing/app.justyo.android](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.justyo.android)
3. Install Yo from Google Play.

Use the same Google account for all three. Once 12 testers have been in for 14 days, Google lets Yo into the Play Store for everyone, so every Android Yo helps.

No phone number, no email: just a username. Every Yo, photo and video is end-to-end encrypted. Not sure who to Yo first? Yo YoGPT.
